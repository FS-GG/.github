## Process problems we can fix

- **Actions queue saturation.** Root measured 183-216 queued runs and 30-60+ minute waits per PR ([#4730 finding](https://github.com/FS-GG/.github/issues/4730#issuecomment-6534726533)). Causes named there: 40-60 checks per PR, body edits re-queueing runs, rebase cycles. Concurrency governance merged in #5001, #5002, #5013, #5022 and #5024; #5004 is approved and held for a quiet window. Scheduled workflows were dropped too (the drift observer ran twice in 8 hours), so a persistent fdev timer now dispatches it. The queue reads **1 queued, 2 in progress now, but the programme is paused**, so the fixes are **not demonstrated**. The real test is the first busy hour after restart. Self-hosted runners were considered and rejected for this public repository.
- **Moved-base and pinned-SHA races.** The moved-base failure hit at least 9 PRs, each costing 20-60 minutes ([finding](https://github.com/FS-GG/.github/issues/4730#issuecomment-6533750304)); #4994 fixed it. Release candidates pinned to `main` were cancelled twice while queued, and the Host publish was refused once when the settlement bot wrote to Authority (about 13 bursts per 4.5 hours). Root worked around both with quiet windows, which freeze every `.github` merge, and held `.github` merges for roughly 90 minutes across the Host release. The durable fixes (pin to the dispatch SHA with an ancestry check; bounded retry in the publisher) are described but I found no dispatched Delivery for them. *Decision (Root):* file both, since 0.114.0 will need another window.
- **Silent revert.** #4977 deleted about 2,530 lines that #4968 had just landed, and main stayed green because the tests were deleted too. The guard needed four PRs (#5008, #5011, #5015, #5019). #5019 is still open, and I saw nothing making the guard required. *Decision:* merge #5019 and require the guard.
- **Census drift and time bombs.** Census drift happened three times in two days (#4991, #5017, #5096), and Root chose an advisory PR check ([#5099](https://github.com/FS-GG/.github/pull/5099), approved, held). Date- or version-dependent tests keep surfacing: #4993, #5007, the 0.3.1 tag collision ([#1092](https://github.com/FS-GG/FS.GG.Coordination/pull/1092)) and the CPython 3.15 rlimit test ([#5018](https://github.com/FS-GG/.github/issues/5018)).
