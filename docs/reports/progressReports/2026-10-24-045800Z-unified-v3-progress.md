## Process problems we can fix

**Repeated preparation outweighs small gates.** The owner-observed pattern is repeated preparation of an approximately 22 KB supervisor around gates whose actual work takes roughly seven to ten seconds. Reconstructing supervision, input identity and review context repeatedly delays the next useful result. This is evidence of setup cost, not a measured token allocation. Root and the execution owner should preserve and reuse unchanged, reviewed supervisor bytes and prepare the complete input census once per source candidate. Every actual operation still needs its own original clock and admission; reusable source is not a reusable grant. The expected saving is plausible but unmeasured.

**Incomplete inputs caused avoidable refusals.** Two preparations encountered Python inputs loaded later than the initial census. They refused before qualifying the intended work. The remedy was to bind the complete immutable input population before launch, preserving both originals and using already retained inputs rather than downloading them again. That is a concrete correction to preparation, not a reason to label failures flaky or loosen identity checks. Root and the SDD owner retain responsibility for verifying the complete closure at the next actual boundary. The accepted third custody preparation is progress, but does not certify the still-pending compile or product operation.

**Compilation has arrived too late in source assembly.** Large F# changes have accumulated across independently reviewed pieces before their combined compile was exercised. That leaves interface, ordering and representation errors to surface after substantial coordination. The practical change is an early, bounded compile checkpoint when the first representative API and actual caller join exists, followed by focused checks as integration changes. The Coordination and SDD owners should apply this within their existing Delivery and resource admission. Earlier model passes remain useful evidence at their declared scope; they must not be presented as compilation or OS proof.

**Independent review is catching substantive defects.** Templates review found real defects and the owner repaired the candidate. BAR review also exposed deadline and known-process custody gaps, followed by the prospective host-policy issue. These reviews prevented weaker claims or inappropriate runtime attempts. Preserve that review value while making each round concrete: one frozen candidate, a complete finding bundle, focused causal checks and a clear return to its same owner. Do not add another reporting ledger or repeatedly rebuild unchanged evidence. The benefit of the recent repairs is demonstrated at source scope; overall elapsed-time or token improvement is not yet measured.

Useful parallelism has improved. Templates, SDD and Rendering can now make independently meaningful source progress while Home and trusted-runtime prerequisites remain held. This reduces dependence on one host response without inventing extra Deliveries or treating blocked sibling work as a prerequisite. Parallel activity still needs disjoint ownership and aggregate resource accounting; occupied agents are not themselves evidence of progress.

## Blockers the current process cannot resolve

**Home needs an executable supplier result.** Root must supply the complete working installed entrypoint and the authenticated OS/receiver interface needed for the intended operation. Home remains stopped until that concrete route exists and qualifies. Additional handoffs, another safety statement or asking the user again for general permission cannot replace implementation. The bounded mail observer is a communication channel, not a running Home workload or renewed runtime grant.

**Some runtime capabilities remain unsupported or unproved.** BAR needs a preconfigured host that meets its literal rootless, namespace, resource and clock gates. D5 still lacks child-level evidence explaining its mapping refusal. Removing a policy setter does not make either host suitable, and parent policy observations cannot prove a common cause. The host supplier and accountable source owners must supply the deciding evidence or an explicitly reviewed prospective contract change. No policy relaxation, rootful fallback or automatic retry is a supported workaround.

**Credentials, original custody and independent machines are external inputs.** UTEL authentication, Keeper access/pointer custody, trusted publication, original unknown effects, the independent KVM allocations, and the FourD/Unity operator responses retain their named suppliers. Their absence cannot be solved by more source workers. Reconsider each at its actual supplier response or capability event; do not release old reservations or infer cleanup from a later absence.
