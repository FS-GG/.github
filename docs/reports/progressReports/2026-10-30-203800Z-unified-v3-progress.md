## Process problems we can fix

- **Review loops: productive but heavy.** Almost every PR needed a repair round, and several needed more: Wizard 0.23.1 ([#4966](https://github.com/FS-GG/.github/pull/4966)) had 9 commits over 97 minutes, [#4968](https://github.com/FS-GG/.github/pull/4968) had 6 commits and 3h20m open to merge, and #4980 has three repair commits. They caught real defects (a false `read-only` label on a merging tool, fail-open parsing, a wrongly described cause of board auto-add). *Effect:* about 1 to 3 hours per PR. *Cause (suspected):* briefs ask workers to land large changes in one PR. *Change (Root):* split PRs by concern before review, which is what #4941's four-PR plan did, and have the author run the exact CI gates before requesting review. The benefit is plausible and unmeasured.
- **Path-filtered silent reds on main.** Three were found: `skill-registry-coherence` (red 6 days), `edge`, `skill-union` (red since 19 October). *Effect:* drift accumulated unseen, then blocked a release gate (the census) at the worst time. *Change:* the main-health observer on [#4730](https://github.com/FS-GG/.github/issues/4730) (scheduled, runs path-filtered checks against main whatever changed). It is dispatched but not yet merged. Fixes landed for all three ([#4962](https://github.com/FS-GG/.github/pull/4962), [#4972](https://github.com/FS-GG/.github/pull/4972), [#4979](https://github.com/FS-GG/.github/pull/4979)).
- **Slow CI and change-completeness timeouts.** The 240 s whole-script budget timed out on #4956, #4968 and three attempts on #4981. Root measured the cold compiler build at 3m39s and found that a failed job never saves its cache, so reruns stay cold. *Change (Root, [#4982](https://github.com/FS-GG/.github/pull/4982)):* save the cache on failure and raise the budget to 420 s. Runner congestion is reported by Root but I found no measured queue-time record, so it is **unmeasured**. Until #4982 lands, expect more cold-cache failures.
- **Board drift observer not yet proven.** [#4973](https://github.com/FS-GG/.github/pull/4973) merged at 18:11 with one successful dry run, which reported 14 findings. The hourly schedule (minute 23) has produced **no scheduled run** since, and I found no "Board drift" issue. GitHub often delays schedules, so this is a watch item, not a defect finding. Three cards were stale for hours this morning, and the board still has one card (#4935) without Status. *Owner:* Root. If no scheduled run appears by the next top of hour, treat it as unarmed.
- **Owner-report claims need checking against the record.** The "400-scenario differential" claim could not be located in the owning issues or PRs. The nearest figures are 600,000 cases ([#4941](https://github.com/FS-GG/.github/issues/4941#issuecomment-6520630433)) and 25,000 scenarios in the #4956 review. I cannot say which was meant. Separately, the earlier report's causes for auto-add and telemetry were both wrong and were corrected by Root the same day, which is the process working. *Change:* have reviewers re-run the headline number, and quote the generator's own count in the closing comment.
- **fdev reboots and reviewer timer noise.** Home rebooted fdev to restore the LEARN profile files, a legitimate interruption. The request also raised reboot interruptions and reviewer timer noise, but **neither is recorded in an owning issue**, so I cannot size them. *Decision needed:* if they recur, file one owned finding with timestamps.
