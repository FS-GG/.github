## 2. Custody recovery and safe continuation

SC2 and BAR retained attempts need their genuine original qualification records. Inspect the
original owner volume, exact recorded paths and authorized byte-preserving backups; match complete
file membership, hashes and original operation identities. A handoff summary, fresh run or
reconstructed receipt is not a replacement. Missing custody remains unknown and fences the
corresponding operation; it need not stop disjoint source inventory or other product work.

Preserve interrupted branches, private evidence, consumed grants and the first failed attempt.
Do not retry an unknown native effect because a process is absent or a timestamp expired. Observe
its original identity through the existing supported route before any separately admitted operation.
The [diagnostic execution policy](../coordination/diagnostic-execution.md) governs budgets, independent
failure collection and cleanup. This roadmap adds no broader host/resource permission.

## 4. Shared lessons and conditional scope

Feed reusable completed work and future product findings into
[Game template consolidation](2026-10-07-unified-development-roadmap-v3.md#3-game-template-consolidation).
Give each finding a producer/template/product/operational disposition with an exact reproducer or
explicit applicability decision. Keep licensed assets, native content, gameplay rules and gateway
permissions out of generic templates. New observations update existing owning plans; they do not
turn product incidents into unsupported shared-framework defect claims.

with its own [owning plan](https://github.com/FS-GG/FSBarV2/blob/main/docs/roadmaps/barc-01-factory-atomic-replacement.md).

product selection. None is a prerequisite for another product's current outcome.

After authoritative closure, record durable evidence in the owning plan and remove or narrow the
corresponding open row here. Do not append completed-work history. Shared producer/template closures
also update the unified open index; games delivery alone does not close those shared outcomes.
