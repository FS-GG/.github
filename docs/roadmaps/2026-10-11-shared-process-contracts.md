release authority or establish cleanup. Nonblocking TryRelease refuses unresolved

and event tick; historical exit/reader facts cannot supply fresh capability/empty-scope
proof. Original deadlines do not renew. Metadata stays within 64 KiB with explicit
omissions and first-cause priority; private terminal/failure history rejects contradictions
without restoring omitted public proof. A cumulative sink snapshot ingests its latched
failure once, while explicit independent failure events remain separate.
