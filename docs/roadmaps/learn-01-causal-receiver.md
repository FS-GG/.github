# LEARN-01.2 causal declaration receiver

This is the receiver-owned C1 source window of LEARN-01.2. The [LEARN owning plan](2026-09-12-095059-stable-policy-orchestration-and-statistical-learning.md) remains the completion ledger. Coordination owns committed admission/1 and producer publication; this repository owns the versioned `execution-causal-admission/1` telemetry event, immutable retention and bounded analysis.

The event binds its canonical identity, item and dispatch to the embedded admission/1 DTO. It carries canonical base64 of the exact decoded committed admission bytes, their SHA-256, and the verified route-binding and launch-intent SHA-256 references. The receiver parses the closed admission shape, bounded purpose/dependency/retry declaration and canonical invocation lineage; reserializing the DTO cannot replace the retained bytes. Declaration edges preserve their recorded order and source references. A source digest is evidence about committed bytes, not execution authority or access to another workspace.

Schema-14 `ingest_facts` retains the event with first-receipt provenance. Exact replay is idempotent; changed content, revision or kind substitution involving a causal declaration refuses atomically. On a receipt-scoped store, the exact immutable causal error terminates the changed receipt as `rejected`/`semantic-conflict`; replay of that receipt returns the same terminal state and leaves the canonical snapshot unchanged. An unknown causal kind is rejected by the parser before a durable receipt exists. The existing `learningObservations` relation exposes the opaque canonical fact under its existing row, byte and selected-item bounds. Older batches and snapshots remain decodable; an older receiver or analyzer may refuse the new kind. No database migration, backfill or existing fact rewrite occurs.

The private analyzer reports declarations returned, selected admitted invocations without declarations, missing or contradictory own joins, declared dependency coverage counts and unresolved references, with bounded diagnostic samples. `dependencyCoverage=complete` describes only a source declaration for that admission. Missing historical facts, parent/root/dependency targets and external-original references remain unresolved. The analyzer keeps `waitCoverage=unknown` and `criticalPath=not-evaluated`; declarations cannot complete the invocation census, usage, capture, allocation, enrollment or token comparison.

The fixed fixtures in `tests/learn-01-analysis/fixtures/causal-admission/` are synthetic. Their manifest pins case count and each admission/event byte length and digest. The root admission intentionally reorders JSON properties and includes whitespace to catch raw-byte reserialization. Manifest route/intent digests are inert contract references, not hashes of supplied committed route/intent files. Coordination's real PostgreSQL controls must derive those digests from genuine retained route/intent bytes and pass emitted batches through an exact qualified receiver artifact.
