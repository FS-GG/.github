# LEARN-01.1 current/focused experiment contract

This source contract freezes a reviewable comparison. It does not enroll work, call a provider, publish
an artifact, change an installed route or adopt a policy. The machine-readable authority is
[`policy/learn-01-current-focused-v1.json`](../../policy/learn-01-current-focused-v1.json); the analyzer
and synthetic corpus exercise its accounting boundary.

## Frozen comparison

The selected class is a routine `.github` source milestone that already has an accepted plan and needs
no protected operation. The control is the repository's current bounded unified-roadmap handoff. The
candidate replaces broad referenced inputs with exact applicable excerpts and identities while retaining
the same mandatory instructions and bounded retrieval. Both arms use one isolated original item, the
same Sol-medium worker, routine process, tools, permissions, decomposition, review and rescue rules.
The contract maps item identity, lineage, requested/observed profile, usage and terminal outcome to today's
native facts. It marks the pre-dispatch rubric, manifest and persisted experiment assignment as the
smallest additive observation contracts for LEARN-01.2 instead of pretending those facts already exist.

Assignment is persisted before treatment work. Children, retries, PRs, rescue and delayed repair inherit
their canonical original item's arm and remain costs and outcomes of that item. The expected root and
descendant invocation and turn inventory is frozen independently of observed usage. Every expected turn
must reconcile to exactly one provider total, and a unique shared cost is allocated once with fractions
summing to one. Provider totals are atomic: cache and reasoning counters describe the total and are not
added to it. A missing expected turn, nonterminal invocation or unbounded usage makes that original item's
token total incomplete; it is never replaced with zero.

The current native collaboration telemetry adapter is **not configured for this workspace**. Therefore
the selected class is not presently eligible for enrollment. The smallest collection prerequisite is a
qualified existing private join that covers every expected root and descendant with terminal
provider-native totals or an explicit unbounded gap. The synthetic analysis remains useful for reviewing
identity, outcome and cost semantics while this prerequisite is absent.

## Window and decision

The frozen prospective window enrolls for at least 28 and at most 84 days, with a 14-day fixed outcome
deadline, 30 days of repair follow-up after acceptance and a 60-day maximum follow-up from assignment.
Only the final lock is inferential. Material model, provider, recipe, permission, process or measurement
drift interrupts the versioned window instead of mixing policies.

The primary target is at least a 10% reduction in the arithmetic mean total tokens per assigned original
item, using a 95% blocked randomization interval for the arithmetic-mean ratio. Legal zero totals remain
in that mean; missing totals remain unknown. Median and geometric-mean summaries are secondary, and the
geometric mean is undefined for an arm containing zero totals. The planning calculation uses coefficient
of variation 2.0, 80% power and design effect 1.1, yielding 6,908 independent original items per arm.
This conservative requirement makes insufficient data an expected honest outcome at low volume.
Fixed-horizon completion and 30-day material repair each have a 10
percentage-point non-inferiority margin; median assignment-to-acceptance latency has a 1.25 ratio margin.
All guardrails and the primary target must pass the joint rule. No subgroup can replace it.

The build and analysis investment is capped at five engineering days, with a scope decision after day
two. That cap is separate from the bounded enrollment and repair-observation calendar.

## Synthetic coverage

Run `bash tests/learn-01-analysis/run.sh`. The corpus contains accepted, failed, cancelled and open work,
a canonical child with a rescue invocation, a delayed repair and one shared cost. Assignment, acceptance,
repair and cutoff times exercise 14-day completion, 30-day repair maturity and open-item censoring. Its
open item has unknown unbounded usage, so
the report must remain unqualified for a token comparison. Tests also prove that the analyzer refuses:

- a baseline label derived from the outcome;
- an invocation or subissue counted as an independent success;
- a missing expected root or child usage observation;
- unbounded missing tokens imputed as zero; and
- shared costs allocated more than once.

The generated numbers are fixture assertions only. They are not an efficiency result or evidence about a
live current/focused population.
