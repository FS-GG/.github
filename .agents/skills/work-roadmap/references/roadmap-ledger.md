# Markdown roadmap ledger

The roadmap's dependency order and checkboxes determine the next milestone. A worker may mark a
milestone complete only after its implementation is merged and its acceptance evidence is recorded.
Preserve milestone text and history; add concise evidence rather than rewriting the plan. Newly
discovered distinct work is added to the roadmap or filed according to its own routing rules before the
worker exits. The completion evidence must identify the critique artifact alongside the PR and
feedback report; minor critique follow-ups must name their durable issue or roadmap item.
